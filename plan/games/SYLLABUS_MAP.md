# Class 9 NCERT -> game map (read from the NCERT PDFs, pdftotext)
Rationalised edition (2023-24 onward): Science has 12 chapters. 1 Matter in Our Surroundings, 2 Is Matter Around Us Pure, 3 Atoms and Molecules, 4 Structure of the Atom, 5 Fundamental Unit of Life, 6 Tissues, 7 Motion, 8 Force and Laws of Motion, 9 Gravitation (includes thrust, pressure, buoyancy, Archimedes), 10 Work and Energy, 11 Sound, 12 Improvement in Food Resources. Removed chapters: Diversity of Living Organisms, Why Do We Fall Ill, Natural Resources. Older PDFs number Work and Energy as 11 and Sound as 12, so cite chapters by title.
Maths has 12 chapters: 1 Number Systems, 2 Polynomials, 3 Coordinate Geometry, 4 Linear Equations in Two Variables, 5 Euclid's Geometry, 6 Lines and Angles, 7 Triangles, 8 Quadrilaterals, 9 Circles, 10 Heron's Formula, 11 Surface Areas and Volumes, 12 Statistics. Removed: Constructions, Probability, and the area of parallelograms and triangles.
