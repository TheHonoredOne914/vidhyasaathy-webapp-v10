questions passed in class

Chits from the back bench.

Q.Does Saathy just make things up?

No. It answers from your teacher's notes and school material, and cites the note. If something isn't covered, it says so.

Q.We share one phone at home. Okay?

Yes. Each child has a separate profile with their own PIN, so progress never mixes.

Q.What if the internet drops?

Chapters, quizzes and games you've synced keep working offline. Answers save on the phone and sync later.

Q.How does a student get an account?

The school prints a claim sheet with a code for each student. Enter it once, set a PIN, done.
