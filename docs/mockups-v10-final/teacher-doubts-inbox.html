Retardation is negative acceleration, right? But in Q12 the book's answer is +2 m/s² for a car that is slowing down. Why is it positive?
Motion · AccelerationSaathy wasn't sure, so the student chose to ask you. You see this question only, not their chat.
Your reply
Good question, Aarav. Retardation is acceleration pointing against the motion. Q12 asks for the size of the retardation, so the book writes 2 m/s². As an acceleration it is −2 m/s². We'll draw the arrows in class tomorrow.
